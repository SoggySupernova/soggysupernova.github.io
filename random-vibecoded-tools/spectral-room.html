Notes

Spectral integration uses an analytic multi-lobe fit of the CIE 1931 2° observer (Wyman et al. 2013) and the sRGB matrix; out-of-gamut colours (e.g. monochromatic light) are clipped. CCT is the nearest Planckian point in CIE 1960 uv; the Ra shown uses the CIE 13.3 procedure but with synthetic approximations of the eight test samples (the same eight patches are on the right wall), so treat it as indicative, not certified. The two “Twin” cubes on the shelf are metamers: identical under D65, different spectra. Surface shading is single-bounce with a flat ambient term.
