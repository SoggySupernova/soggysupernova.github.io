Notes

Spectra live on a 300–780 nm grid in 5 nm steps. Human observers use the tabulated CIE 1931 2°, CIE 1964 10° and CIE 2015 (“2012”) cone-fundamental-based colour matching functions, converted to sRGB with the standard matrix, and each observer is calibrated so CIE D65 shows as display white. Out-of-gamut colours (e.g. monochromatic light) are clipped. CCT, Duv, the CIE diagram and CRI always use the CIE 1931 2° observer; the CRI follows CIE 13.3 with the real CIE 1995 test-colour spectra TCS01–08 (the eight patches on the right wall) and matches colour-science to ±0.2. Dichromats use Stockman–Sharpe cone fundamentals and rods use CIE 1951 V′. Animal receptors are pigment templates (Govardovskii 2000) at published λmax values with rough oil-droplet filters for birds: approximate, and a 2- to 4-receptor visual space can only be shown through a 3-primary screen. Twin A / Twin B are metamers under D65 for the 2° observer. Shading is single-bounce with a flat ambient term.
